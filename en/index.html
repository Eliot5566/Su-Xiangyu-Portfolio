Paper Radar

Reads every new arXiv paper each morning and surfaces the few you should actually read.

  • 33 sto judge 501 papers
  • $0.06a day to read all of arXiv
  • 96.9%systematic-review recall
  • Write your interests in plain English; the model answers one yes/no question per paper with a calibrated probability, and code decides must-read, maybe or skip.
  • On four unseen Cochrane reviews it screened 19,447 records, recovered 96.9% of the studies reviewers included and cut reading by 78%.
  • Runs entirely on GitHub Actions + Pages with a web page and RSS feed, plus optional Slack, Discord and Telegram digests. Fork and go, no server.
